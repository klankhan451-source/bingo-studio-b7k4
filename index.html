📖 Quick guide

  1. Create: pick a game type and theme (or paste your own list — any language works), choose how many cards, a design, and Generate.
  2. Print: use “Print / Save PDF”. Print at 100% scale. The pack includes a cover, how-to-play page, the cards, calling cards, a master call sheet and cut-out slips.
  3. Play: open the Live Caller tab — it calls in the same order as the printed sheet, can read calls aloud, and keeps the board for you.
  4. Check winners: every card has a printed Card ID. Enter it in the Card Checker — because sets are seed-based, the exact card is rebuilt and verified.
  5. Selling your packs? Your licence covers selling the finished bingo sets you create (see the Commercial Use Licence in your download). The tool and your code themselves must never be shared or resold.

Trouble printing? Try the Ink Saver design, check the paper size matches your printer, and keep “Background graphics” ON if a design uses coloured headers.
